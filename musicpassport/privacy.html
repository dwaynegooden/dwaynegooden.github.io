Music Passport privacy policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Music Passport (including Music Passport Lite) is an app made by Dwayne Gooden (NeoPulse Solutions) that keeps your liked songs and chosen playlists in step across music streaming services. This page explains what it reads, what it stores and why.

What the app reads from your music services

  • Spotify, YouTube Music, Apple Music: with your permission, the app reads your liked songs, your playlists and song details (title, artist, album, length, and the song's ISRC code), and searches the service's catalog to find the same song.
  • What it writes: only what you set up: it adds songs to your likes and to playlists you chose, and creates "(synced)" playlist copies. It never deletes songs or playlists.
  • The app uses this data only to sync your library and show where songs are available. It is not used for advertising or any other purpose.

Sign-ins

Your sign-in tokens are stored only on your phone (in its secure storage) or, for the full version, on your own home server (NAS). They are never sent to the developer or to the app's cloud database.

What the app stores

  • On your device / your server: a record of your libraries, song matches, sync history and settings, so syncing can resume where it stopped.
  • Cloud database (optional, full version): song-matching details only (track ids, titles, artists, match decisions and sync status), stored with Supabase (United States) and protected so only your account can read it. No passwords, tokens or payment details.
  • App log: an on-device diagnostics log. It stays on your phone unless you choose to share it.

Google and YouTube data

Music Passport's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is accessed under the YouTube Terms of Service and handled as described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

What the app does not do

  • No ads, no tracking, no selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No human reads your library; there is no analytics service.

Deleting your data

Sign out of a service in Settings, Accounts to remove its tokens from your phone, and uninstall the app to delete its on-device data. You can also revoke access in each service's account settings. Email the address below and any cloud data tied to your account will be deleted.
