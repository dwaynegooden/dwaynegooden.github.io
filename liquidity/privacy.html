LiquIDity privacy policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

LiquIDity is a home-bar inventory app made by Dwayne Gooden (NeoPulse Solutions). This page explains what the app stores and why.

What the app stores

  • Your bar: bottles, fill levels, prices you enter, lists, notes and the photos you take. These stay on your phone. If you sign in, they also sync to the app's private cloud database so you can use another phone and share with people you invite.
  • Your account: when you sign in with Google, Apple or email, the app receives your name, email address and profile picture. It uses them only to identify you and show your name to people in your sharing circle.
  • Shared catalog: bottles you choose to share (name, details, cut-out photo, 3D model) are visible to members of your circle.
  • Google Drive backup (optional): if you turn it on, the app saves a backup file to a private app folder in your own Google Drive. Only the app can see that folder; the developer cannot.

What the app does not do

  • No ads, no tracking, no selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • Optional AI features run on your phone. Your photos are not sent to an AI service.

Where data is kept

Cloud data is stored with Supabase (United States) and protected so that only you and the people you invite can see it.

Deleting your data

You can delete bottles, photos and your account from the app's settings, or email the address below and everything tied to your account will be deleted.
